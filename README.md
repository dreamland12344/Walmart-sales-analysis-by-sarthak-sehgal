Walmart Sales Analysis
Project Overview

This project analyzes Walmart's weekly sales data using SQL and Tableau to identify sales trends, compare store performance, and generate actionable business insights.

Tools Used
SQL (Google BigQuery): Data analysis, aggregation, and business queries.
Tableau: Interactive dashboard and data visualization.
Key Business Questions
Which stores generate the highest and lowest sales?
How do weekly sales change over time?
Do holiday weeks generate higher sales than non-holiday weeks?
Is there a relationship between sales and temperature, fuel prices, CPI, and unemployment?
Which stores have the most consistent sales?
Key Findings
Store 20 had the highest reported total sales.
Store 33 had the lowest reported total sales.
Holiday dates showed higher average total sales than non-holiday dates in the report.
Temperature and fuel prices showed weak relationships with weekly sales.
Store performance and seasonal trends can help guide business decisions.

Business Recommendations
Investigate the reasons for lower sales at Store 33.
Benchmark store performance against Store 20.
Plan inventory and staffing around seasonal sales peaks.
Monitor weekly sales and holiday performance through dashboards.
